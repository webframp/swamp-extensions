**File:** `scripts/cloudflare-codegen/bump.ts` (`requireNotesForRepin`, `updatePinnedSha`), `scripts/falai-codegen/lib/bump.ts` (`requireNotesForRepin`, `updateSpec`) **Line(s):** the guard and the config write
**Issue:** The guard only checks that a notes flag is present, not that it is usable. The comment's promise that "a run never leaves it moved with nothing regenerated" is not true.
**Breaking input:** `deno task bump -- --update-sha --notes-file missing.md`, `--update-sha --notes ""`, or `--update-spec --version &lt;older than the upgrade chain&gt; --notes x`.
**Impact:** `config.ts` (and falai's vendored spec) are rewritten. Then `main.ts` fails on the missing file, the empty-notes refusal or the version rejection, and the pin is left moved. Recovery is `git checkout`.
**Fix:** Before re-pinning, check that a `--notes-file` exists and that `--notes` is not blank. Or snapshot `config.ts` and the spec, and restore them when the re-exec exits non-zero.
