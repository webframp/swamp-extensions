### [LOW] "Keys from `list` stay readable" is an overclaim
**File:** `vault/pass/RELEASE_NOTES.md` (second **Added:** block); `vault/pass/README.md` ("…and every key `list` returns stays readable. A key such as `smtp#login` is always read as a field selection, though…")
**Issue:** An entry created outside swamp, or before the separator was set, whose name ends in `#&lt;valid-field-name&gt;` is listed but cannot be read: `get` reads a field of the shorter entry.
